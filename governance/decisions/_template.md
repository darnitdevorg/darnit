## Context

What forced a decision: the real constraints, not the aspirational ones.

## Scope

How the decision passes ADR-0001's admission test: what setup it reads or
changes, why its answer stays the same when the setup does not change, and
what that exists today needs it.

## Decision

What was decided, in the fewest words that are still unambiguous.

## Alternatives

What else was considered, and the specific reason each lost. "Simpler" is not
a reason; say what the alternative could not do.

## Consequences

What this costs, including what it makes hard. An ADR with only upsides was not
a decision.

## Enforcement

The `ENF-` id and its row in `ENFORCEMENT.md`, or a statement that this ADR
creates no rule. Most ADRs create no rule.
